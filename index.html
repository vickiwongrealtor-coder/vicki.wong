成交案例 — Deals That Required More Than a Listing

並非每一個物業都能自己賣出。這些案例，策略、數據與人脈缺一不可。

Case Study 01

存量資產重構 — Repositioning a Stalled Listing

Challenge
A San Jose single-family home with an ADU sat unsold for months — buyers couldn't see the ADU's long-term value.
Strategy
Pivoted away from emotional buyers to a curated list of investors focused on cash flow and appreciation.
Outcome
Matched with a private investor and closed quickly, bypassing the open market entirely.
Case Study 02

資產溢價錨定 — Reframing a Multi-Building Property

Challenge
A rare multi-building property in Mountain View sat on the market for nearly six months; the owner was ready to rent it out instead.
Strategy
Repositioned the listing around its cap rate and investment scarcity, reaching buyers who saw it as an asset, not just a home.
Outcome
Closed under rental-deadline pressure — at one of the neighborhood's highest recorded prices.

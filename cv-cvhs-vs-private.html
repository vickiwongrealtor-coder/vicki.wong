The Measure B Impact on School Quality

The 2026 Measure B bond represents the largest single investment in CVUSD infrastructure in a generation. The funds are designated for aging classroom modernization, technology infrastructure upgrades, seismic safety improvements, and new STEM lab facilities. For families choosing Castro Valley specifically for the schools, Measure B signals that the community is doubling down on what makes the district exceptional.

Understanding School Zone Boundaries

The most important thing I tell every family buyer in Castro Valley: don't assume the address gets you the school you want. CVUSD boundary lines are drawn at the street level, and two homes on the same block can feed into different elementary schools with very different performance profiles. I verify school zone assignments for every buyer before we write an offer — it's one of the most consequential due diligence steps in this market.

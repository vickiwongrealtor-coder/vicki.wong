The Trails You'll Never Find Without a Local

The Five Canyons Perimeter Loop is the crown jewel of Castro Valley hiking — 5 miles, 800 feet of elevation, and views of the Bay that stretch from Mount Tamalpais to the Santa Cruz Mountains on clear days. The East Shore of Lake Chabot is a fraction as crowded as the main trail and arguably more beautiful.

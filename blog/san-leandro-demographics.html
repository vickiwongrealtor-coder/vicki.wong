Property crime slightly above CA average; violent crime near CA average. East of I-880 suburban areas significantly safer than BART corridor.

⚠️ How to use crime data correctly: Estudillo Estates, Washington Manor, and Davis/Bancroft neighborhoods are among safest in Alameda County. Avoid over-indexing on citywide stats — micro-neighborhood matters enormously here. Citywide crime statistics are averages that mask enormous neighborhood-level variation. A single city can contain areas with crime rates 5x higher and 5x lower than the city average. Always research your specific target address using the local police department's public crime mapping tool. Parks, Open Space & Recreation

The Bay Area's outdoor access is one of its most underappreciated assets — and one of the primary reasons long-term residents stay despite high housing costs. San Leandro residents have access to:

  • San Leandro Marina Park — bay access, boat ramp, fishing pier, 2.5 miles of shoreline trails
  • Davis Park — community center, sports fields, playground
  • Estudillo Park — historic neighborhood park
  • San Leandro Creek Trail — 12.5 miles connecting to regional network
  • Anthony Chabot Regional Park — 5,069 acres, 5 minutes east (East Bay's largest park)
  • Lake Chabot — fishing, kayaking, hiking, 10 minutes

For families with dogs, the East Bay Regional Park District (for East Bay cities) and Peninsula Open Space Trust properties provide trail access that most US metro areas cannot match. I factor park access into neighborhood recommendations for buyers who've indicated it as a priority — which is most of the families I work with.

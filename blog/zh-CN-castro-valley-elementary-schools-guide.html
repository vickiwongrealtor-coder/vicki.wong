What This Means for Home Buyers

School zone boundaries in Castro Valley are not always intuitive. Two homes on the same street can feed into different elementary schools depending on exact address. Always verify the school assignment directly with CVUSDbefore making an offer.

💡Homes in the Jensen Ranch 入学范围 consistently sell faster and for more per square footthan comparable properties in other CVUSD zones. If top-tier elementary academics are a non-negotiable, budget accordingly — and be prepared to compete.

2026 Market Context: Geopolitics, Gas & AI Economy

Three forces define Berkeley's 2026 market. Geopolitical instability drives flight-to-safety capital into Bay Area premium markets. California gas at $4.50–$6.00/gallon makes BART access a measurable price premium. The AI economy: Very strong — UC Berkeley and Lawrence Lab are foundational global AI research institutions. Berkeley is where many AI researchers live and work.. The rate lock-in effect (millions holding 2020–2022 mortgages at 2.5%–3.5%) keeps inventory historically tight.

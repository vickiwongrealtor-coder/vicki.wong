Why People Buy & Stay 為何買家留下來

Stanford, schools, and staying power

帕羅奧圖是史丹福大學所在地，亦擁有加州數一數二的公立學區，吸引許多打算長期扎根的家庭。

PAUSD — California's top district 學區之首

Palo Alto Unified consistently ranks among the strongest public school systems in the state, drawing families years ahead of enrollment.

Stanford at the doorstep 史丹福鄰接

Proximity to Stanford University brings a dense concentration of researchers, founders, and academics into the housing pool.

Safe, walkable, and clean 安全宜居

Downtown Palo Alto and its neighborhoods are consistently rated among the safest, best-kept communities in the Bay Area.

Deep, durable equity 保值穩健

Decades of constrained supply against relentless demand have made Palo Alto one of the most resilient housing markets in the country.

Famous Houses in Palo Alto

HP Garage — photo
HP Garage
367 Addison Ave — the one-car garage where Bill Hewlett and Dave Packard founded HP in 1939, now a California Historical Landmark known as the "Birthplace of Silicon Valley."
Steve Jobs House — photo
Steve Jobs House
Jobs' longtime Waverley Street home, a Tudor Revival house he lived in until his passing in 2011 — still a quiet pilgrimage site for Apple admirers.

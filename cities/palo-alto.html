Why Families Choose This Area

Palo Alto Unified School District is one of the most academically distinguished public school districts in the United States. Gunn High School and Palo Alto High School both regularly place students in Ivy League and top-10 universities. For families where education outcomes are the primary consideration, PAUSD is hard to beat.

Beyond schools, Palo Alto offers a genuine downtown with excellent restaurants, walkable neighborhoods, and unparalleled proximity to Google, Meta, and hundreds of venture-funded startups. It's a city that rewards long-term ownership — prices here have appreciated consistently for decades.

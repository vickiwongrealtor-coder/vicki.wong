Why People Buy & Stay 為何買家留下來

Variety, transit, and community — at every price point

聖荷西是矽谷之都，作為灣區最大城市，擁有多元的社區、價位與族裔社群供買家選擇。

Silicon Valley's biggest job base 最大就業腹地

As the largest city in the Bay Area, San Jose puts buyers within easy reach of the region's largest concentration of tech employers.

Neighborhood-by-neighborhood variety 社區選擇多元

From West San Jose's top-rated schools to Downtown's urban core, the city offers a wider range of price points than most of the Peninsula.

Best transit access in the South Bay 交通便利

Caltrain, VTA light rail, and Highways 101/280/880/17 converge here, making it the easiest South Bay city to commute from in any direction.

Diverse, family-oriented communities 多元家庭社區

Large, established Chinese and Vietnamese communities give newcomers an easy landing — from grocery stores to community associations.

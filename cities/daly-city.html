Why People Buy & Stay 為何買家留下來

San Francisco access without San Francisco pricing

戴利城緊鄰三藩市，擁有全美數一數二的菲律賓社區，並設有兩個 BART 站。

Right on the San Francisco line 緊鄰三藩市

Daly City borders San Francisco directly, giving buyers city-adjacent access at a meaningfully lower price point.

One of the largest Filipino communities in the U.S. 全美最大菲律賓社區之一

Daly City is home to one of the largest concentrations of Filipino Americans in the country, with deep cultural and religious infrastructure.

Lower violent crime than the national average 暴力罪案率低

Violent crime runs notably below the national rate, even though overall cost-of-living pressure is high.

BART at both ends of the city 雙 BART 站

Daly City and Colma BART stations offer a direct line into San Francisco without driving through it.

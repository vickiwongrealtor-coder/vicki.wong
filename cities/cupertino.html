Why People Buy & Stay 為何買家留下來

Schools, safety, and Apple — a rare combination

庫比蒂諾是矽谷中一個規模精巧、供應穩定的城市，以蘋果總部為核心。許多家庭因學區而置業，因社區穩定而留下。

Apple HQ economy 蘋果總部經濟圈

Apple Park anchors the city with roughly 12,000 employees and dozens of nearby offices, keeping demand for housing steady even in soft markets.

Fremont Union schools 學區王牌

Monta Vista and Cupertino High feed some of the strongest STEM outcomes in the state — a top draw for families relocating for education.

One of the safest cities of its size 治安穩定

Violent crime runs about 69% below the national average; residents describe the city as quiet, walkable, and well maintained.

Compact, stable footprint 供應有限

At just 13 square miles with little room for new construction, resale inventory stays tight — a reason values have held firm for decades.

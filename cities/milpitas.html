Why Families Choose This Area

Milpitas has one of the most concentrated Chinese-speaking populations in the South Bay — Cantonese and Mandarin communities are well-established here, with Chinese supermarkets, restaurants, and cultural institutions throughout the city. The BART station makes commuting to downtown San Jose, Fremont, and Oakland easy.

Santa Clara sits between San Jose and Sunnyvale — tech employers on every side, including Intel and NVIDIA headquarters nearby. It offers better pricing than Sunnyvale with comparable access to Silicon Valley employment centers.

Why People Buy & Stay 為何買家留下來

The entry point — value, diversity, and BART access

海沃德是我們服務的東灣城市中入門門檻最低的一個，社區族裔多元，並設有兩個 BART 站。

The best value in the East Bay 東灣最佳性價比

Hayward consistently offers the lowest entry price among nearby East Bay cities, making it a first stop for many first-time buyers.

Genuinely diverse community 多元族裔社區

Hayward's mix of Hispanic, Asian, and Black communities gives it one of the highest diversity ratings in the East Bay.

Cal State East Bay anchor 大學社區

The university brings steady rental demand and a younger population mix to parts of the city.

Improving transit and downtown investment 交通與市中心發展

Two BART stations and ongoing downtown revitalization are gradually reshaping parts of the city.

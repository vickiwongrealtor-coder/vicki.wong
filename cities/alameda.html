Why People Buy & Stay 為何買家留下來

Small-town streets, big-city access

阿拉米達是一個擁有維多利亞式街道與優質學區的島嶼城市，並設有渡輪直達三藩市，享有小鎮氛圍卻鄰近兩大城市。

An island with small-town character 島嶼小鎮氛圍

Alameda's island geography and Victorian-lined streets give it a tight-knit, small-town feel rarely found this close to Oakland and San Francisco.

Highly rated public schools 學校評級優異

Alameda Unified consistently ranks among the stronger East Bay districts, a major draw for relocating families.

Ferry and bridge access to SF 渡輪直達三藩市

A direct ferry to San Francisco's Ferry Building, alongside two bridges/tubes into Oakland, gives residents flexible commute options.

A genuinely walkable, small-business downtown 可步行的市中心

Park Street and Webster Street support a dense strip of independent restaurants and shops residents describe as central to daily life.

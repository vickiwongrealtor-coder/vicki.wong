Why People Buy & Stay 為何買家留下來

Diversity, safety, and a direct line to BART

聯合市是東灣一個真正多元的城市，治安優於全國平均，設有 BART 站，高學歷居民比例持續上升。

Lower crime than the national average 治安優於全國平均

Union City runs well below the national violent-crime rate, giving buyers East Bay affordability without the safety trade-off of some neighboring cities.

One of the Bay Area's most diverse cities 族裔多元

A large Filipino, Asian, and South Asian population supports deep grocery, temple, and community infrastructure.

BART-connected, freeway central 交通樞紐

Union City BART plus direct access to I-880 and Hwy 92 make it one of the more flexible commute bases in the East Bay.

Above-average education levels 教育水平高於預期

Roughly 45% of adults hold a bachelor's degree or higher — notably high for an entry-to-mid East Bay price point.

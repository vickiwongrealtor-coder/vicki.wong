Why People Buy & Stay 為何買家留下來

Historic character with room to grow into

聖利安卓是一個具歷史風情的東灣城市，設有兩個 BART 站，相比鄰近的阿拉米達或奧克蘭提供更寬敞的居住空間。

Historic charm, real character 歷史風情

Downtown San Leandro retains its historic architecture, giving the city a warmth and identity that newer East Bay suburbs lack.

Two BART stations 兩個 BART 站

San Leandro and Bay Fair BART give residents a direct line to Oakland and San Francisco without a car.

More home for the money 更實惠的居住空間

Compared to Alameda or Oakland, San Leandro consistently offers larger lots and lower price-per-square-foot.

Crime near the state average 治安接近全州平均

Overall crime sits close to both the California and national averages — a middle-of-the-road profile among East Bay cities.

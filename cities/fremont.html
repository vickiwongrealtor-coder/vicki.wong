Why People Buy & Stay 為何買家留下來

A top school zone with deep Chinese community roots

費利蒙以搶手的 Mission San Jose 學區及灣區最大的華人社區之一著稱，吸引重視治安與文化熟悉感的家庭。

Mission San Jose schools 名校學區

The Mission San Jose district is one of the most sought-after school zones in the East Bay, drawing buyers specifically for its boundaries.

One of the Bay Area's largest Chinese communities 華人社區龐大

Fremont has one of the biggest and most established Chinese-American communities in the region, with deep grocery, cultural, and religious infrastructure.

Family-safe, low-key pace 家庭友善

Residents consistently describe Fremont as quiet and safe enough to let kids walk to school — a trade-off for a lower-key nightlife scene.

Central Bay Area access 地理位置優越

Positioned between the South Bay and East Bay, Fremont gives commuters flexible access to both job markets via I-880 and Hwy 84.

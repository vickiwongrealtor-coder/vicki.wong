Why People Buy & Stay 為何買家留下來

New construction, good schools, direct BART

都柏林是東灣一個快速發展的規劃社區，擁有較新建的房屋、口碑良好的學區及直達的 BART 交通。

Newer housing stock 新建房屋

Dublin's master-planned developments mean buyers get newer construction, modern layouts, and fewer deferred-maintenance surprises than older East Bay stock.

Dublin USD's reputation 學區口碑良好

Dublin Unified is one of the more highly regarded East Bay districts, a major draw for relocating families.

BART at both ends of town 兩個 BART 站

Dublin/Pleasanton BART gives residents a direct line into the East Bay and San Francisco without a car.

A fast-growing Asian community 亞裔社區快速增長

Dublin has one of the fastest-growing Asian populations in the East Bay, supporting a growing base of Asian grocers, restaurants, and services.

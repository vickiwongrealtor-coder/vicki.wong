Why Families Choose Mountain View

Mountain View occupies a rare position in the South Bay — it's one of the few Silicon Valley cities with a genuinely walkable downtown (Castro Street), a strong rental market for investors, and proximity to Google, LinkedIn, and dozens of other major tech employers without the premium price tag of Palo Alto or Los Altos.

The city attracts a younger, more diverse demographic than its neighboring South Bay cities. Tech workers in their 30s and 40s, international families, and investors all compete for limited inventory here — which is why homes in desirable Mountain View neighborhoods consistently receive multiple offers.

Neighborhoods Worth Knowing

  • Old Mountain View / Castro Street corridor — most walkable, close to Caltrain, highest demand
  • Cuesta Park / Rex Manor — family-friendly, larger lots, top-rated schools
  • Waverly Park — quiet, established, popular with long-term owners
  • North Whisman / Middlefield Road — newer condos and townhomes, entry-level South Bay price point
  • Shoreline West — close to Google campus, popular with tech employees

Investment Perspective

Mountain View has strong fundamentals for investors. The combination of Google's long-term campus presence, Caltrain connectivity, and a chronically constrained housing supply creates consistent upward pressure on both rents and prices. ADU (Accessory Dwelling Unit) regulations in Mountain View are relatively favorable, making it a popular market for buyers looking to add rental income.

Why People Buy & Stay 為何買家留下來

Tech jobs, top schools, walkable living

山景城是谷歌總部所在地，擁有可步行的市中心與表現優異的學校，深受科技家庭青睞。

Google's hometown 谷歌總部

Google's sprawling campus and a dense cluster of tech employers keep housing demand consistently strong.

Schools that outperform expectations 學校表現優異

Local schools average an A+ SchoolGrade, with actual student proficiency well above what’s statistically projected for the area.

Walkable downtown, younger energy 年輕活力

Castro Street's restaurants and shops give Mountain View a livelier, more walkable feel than many South Bay peers.

Highly educated resident base 高學歷居民

Roughly three-quarters of adult residents hold a bachelor's degree or higher, with a large share holding graduate degrees.

Why People Buy & Stay 為何買家留下來

Every neighborhood is its own decision

三藩市是灣區的文化與經濟核心，居民教育水平高、交通便利，選對社區與選對城市同樣重要。

The Bay Area's cultural and economic core 灣區文化經濟核心

San Francisco anchors the region’s finance, tech, and culture — for many buyers, proximity to the city itself is the primary draw.

One of the most educated cities in the country 全國教育水平最高城市之一

Well over half of adult residents hold a bachelor's degree or higher, among the highest rates of any major U.S. city.

Neighborhood-by-neighborhood diversity 社區差異極大

From family-oriented Sunset and Richmond to dense, transit-rich SoMa and Nob Hill, San Francisco offers dramatically different lifestyles within city limits.

Unmatched transit access 交通網絡完善

BART, Muni, and Caltrain give San Francisco residents the best car-free mobility in the Bay Area.

Why People Buy & Stay 為何買家留下來

A quiet, affordable pocket between two bigger cities

聖羅倫佐是位於海沃德和聖利安卓之間的一個寧靜小鎮，是不追求獨立市中心的買家的實惠入門選擇。

Quiet, close-knit, residential 寧靜社區

San Lorenzo is a small, mostly owner-occupied community — residents describe it as a quiet, friendly town rather than a destination in itself.

Schools that outperform expectations 學校表現超出預期

Local schools average an A- SchoolGrade, with actual proficiency well above statistical projections for the area.

One of the more affordable Alameda County entry points 阿拉米達郡較實惠的入門選擇

Home prices run meaningfully below Alameda, San Leandro, and points north — a common first stop for East Bay buyers.

Everything nearby, without the density 鄰近一切所需

Sitting directly between Hayward and San Leandro, residents reach dining, shopping, and BART in either direction within minutes.

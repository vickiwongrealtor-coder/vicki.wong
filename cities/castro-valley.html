Why People Buy & Stay 為何買家留下來

Space, schools, and a straight shot to BART

卡斯特羅谷是東灣一個連接 BART 的社區，以優質學區、較大居住空間及緊密的家庭社群聞名。

BART in town 直達 BART

A dedicated Castro Valley BART station puts San Francisco and Oakland within an easy, car-free commute.

Strong, established schools 學區穩健

Castro Valley Unified is well regarded, and Castro Valley High holds a 97% graduation rate with broad AP offerings.

More space for the money 更寬敞的居住空間

Compared to the Peninsula and South Bay, Castro Valley offers noticeably larger lots and homes at a lower price per square foot.

A distinct, family-first identity 家庭氛圍濃厚

Residents describe it as a pocket of the Bay Area with its own character — quieter, more conservative, and tightly family-oriented.

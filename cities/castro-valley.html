Why Families Choose This Area

Castro Valley sits at the intersection of convenience and community. It's 35 minutes from San Francisco via BART, 20 minutes from Oakland, and a straight shot to Silicon Valley. The Castro Valley Unified School District (CVUSD) is consistently rated among the top districts in Alameda County.

Jensen Ranch Elementary ranks in the top 2% of all California elementary schools — and homes in its attendance zone command a premium. The community has a growing Cantonese and Mandarin-speaking population, making it a natural choice for Chinese-speaking families.

Castro Valley Elementary Schools:
Complete Breakdown of Rankings, Demographics & Academics

By Vicki Wong May 2026 12 min read Data: 2024–25 School Year

For families considering a move to Castro Valley, the school question comes up in nearly every conversation I have. And rightfully so — Castro Valley Unified School District (CVUSD) is one of the primary reasons this community commands a premium over neighboring Hayward. But not all Castro Valley schools are equal, and where your child is zoned matters significantly.

This guide breaks down all 9 elementary schools in the district by student body demographics, academic test scores, and what the data actually tells you as a parent. I've also added my own on-the-ground perspective from working with families in this community.

Castro Valley Unified operates entirely within unincorporated Alameda County. The district serves a diverse population and elementary schools in Castro Valley score 57% on proficiency tests on average against a projected 43% — significantly exceeding expectations for schools of their socioeconomic profile. All 9 elementary schools eventually feed into Canyon Middle or Creekside Middle, then Castro Valley High School.

What This Means for Home Buyers

School zone boundaries in Castro Valley are not always intuitive. Two homes on the same street can feed into different elementary schools depending on exact address. Always verify the school assignment directly with CVUSD before making an offer.

💡 Homes in the Jensen Ranch attendance zone consistently sell faster and for more per square foot than comparable properties in other CVUSD zones. If top-tier elementary academics are a non-negotiable, budget accordingly — and be prepared to compete.

How to Check Your School Zone

  • Visit cvusd.us and use the school locator tool with the property address
  • Contact the CVUSD district office directly: (510) 537-3000
